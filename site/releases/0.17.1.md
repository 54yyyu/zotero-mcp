---
title: OpenCode in the panel, and {Mermaid diagrams}
date: Nov 2026
---
The Zotero Agent panel runs OpenCode as a built-in agent next to Claude Code, Codex and pi, on OpenCode's free models or your own providers, with no bridge or Node.js to install. Mermaid flowcharts and sequence diagrams in an answer are drawn as figures in the panel's own style, in light and dark, and can be saved to a note. On Windows, Claude Code installed through npm starts again instead of failing with `spawn EINVAL`.

Batch embeddings (OpenAI and Gemini) now index passages too, ending in the same index a realtime update builds. If you already had chunking and batch both on, your next `update-db` embeds passages for the whole library, and the provider bills for it. `zotero-mcp setup` asks for your WebDAV settings in web mode and keeps them on a re-run, and copied items' files go to WebDAV as well.
