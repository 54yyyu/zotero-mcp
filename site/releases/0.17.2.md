---
title: Annotation tools for the CLI, and {named chats}
date: Nov 2026
---
`zotero-cli` can now move a box in place (`annotations update --rect`), list where each annotation is stored (`list --positions`), draw the stored annotations on page images (`annotations preview`, and `batch --dry-run --render` for a plan before anything is written), re-run a batch without duplicating it (`--on-existing`), split a figure into panels, grid a page image, and say which nearby text it found when a highlight's words are not in the PDF. `add doi --attach-file` adds a paper and attaches your own PDF in one call, and `add folder` does it for a folder of PDFs.

Zotero Agent chats are named, by the agent's own summary, the paper you had open or your first question, and you can rename them. Whether sending a message scrolls to the newest one is a setting now. `python -m zotero_mcp` starts the CLI without the `.exe` launcher that Windows Smart App Control can block, and `ZOTERO_ONNX_THREADS` quiets the thread-pinning errors of the default embedding model in containers.
