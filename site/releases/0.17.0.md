---
title: Articles and passages apart, and the {panel in your language}
date: Nov 2026
---
The semantic index now keeps one record per article and the passages of each paper in separate collections. An article search no longer has to load every passage, turning chunking on no longer needs a rebuild, and `zotero_semantic_search(level="articles"|"passages")` picks the level. An index built with chunking on keeps working as it is; `zotero-mcp migrate-index` splits it when you choose to, renaming the passages and embedding nothing until the next update. Automatic updates never fill passages for the whole library on their own: that waits for an `update-db` you run, so a paid embedding API is not billed unasked.

The Zotero Agent panel speaks Chinese (simplified and traditional), Japanese, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, following Zotero's own language unless you pick one, and in Zotero's own words for items, collections and annotations. You can run your own ACP agent next to Claude Code, Codex and pi, Codex chats use your installed Codex when it is newer than the bridge's, and the top bar lines up with the reader toolbar. Items can be copied between libraries (opt-in), Zotero's retraction flags show up in item metadata, exported bibliographies show `&` instead of `&amp;`, and scanned PDFs whose OCR text sits under a visible stamp read correctly.
