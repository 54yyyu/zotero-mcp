We project the molecular dynamics (MD) simulation ensembles onto a two-dimensional plane defined by the radius of gyration (RG) and the root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) relative to the crystal structure. We then compute the Gaussian kernel density of this 2D map and apply the Boltzmann Equation to convert the density values into ‘approximate energy’ values after normalization.
